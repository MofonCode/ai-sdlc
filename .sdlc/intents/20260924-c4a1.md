# Charter review — the ten blocking findings from the first interrogation

## What is wrong

The first interrogation of this charter returned ten blocking findings, every one of them
cited and eight of them a self-contradiction between two passages of this document. They
are listed below with the citation each earned.

The charter cannot be edited in an ordinary cycle, so this intent exists to carry the edit
and to be approved by a person (INV-08).

## What changes

`.sdlc/CHARTER.md` only, to version 2. No invariant is deleted and no ID is reused.
INV-03, INV-06 and the `form` role are reworded; INV-06 keeps its ID because its reach is
unchanged — a `NoWarn` is a warning suppression — and only the stack-specific spelling
moved out of the rule into illustrative evidence.

Two of the ten were genuine design choices rather than wording fixes, and the option
taken is recorded here so a later reader can see it was a decision:

- **r9** — escalation was made an *event* rather than a promotion, so nothing reaches
  `blocking` uncited and the findings schema's anti-inflation rule stays intact. The
  alternative was a sentinel citation kind, which would have widened what `cites` means
  and left an invariant looking cited when nothing real pointed at it.
- **r12** — the coverage matrix was **deleted** rather than repaired and bound with a
  check. It was derived data in a hand-maintained document with no generator, so deleting
  it removes the second list instead of promising to keep it in step.

## What this intent does not do

`r39` requires the zero-cite health query to verify a `Prevented-by:` target before
reporting "working as designed". This intent states that requirement in the charter; the
query itself does not exist yet and is deferred. That is deliberate: stating the
requirement is what stops the field becoming a permanent unfalsifiable pass, and building
the query is not a charter change.

The 20 `suggested` findings from the same interrogation are not addressed here. They
accumulate as evidence and are triaged separately, per the charter's timing rule.
